Nathan (he/him) is a double major in Political Science and Anthropology, with a specific passion for sociocultural anthropology and civil rights, expecting to graduate in Spring 2029. He is interested in researching civic and environmental policy, an interest that developed through personal experiences with community service and environmental research back home in the Bay Area. Nathan is keen on exploring and identifying gaps between how policies are designed and how they operate in practice. Through this, he hopes to help raise awareness of the less visible effects of policy that may create barriers for certain communities. In his free time, Nathan enjoys films, video games, comic books, and playing D&D with friends. As a member of the Washington Policy Initiative, Nathan hopes to grow as a researcher, writer, and advocate for others.
