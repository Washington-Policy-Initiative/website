Claire is a Public Policy and Service major at the University of Washington and serves as the Director of Communications for the Washington Policy Initiative. Her academic interests include international development, political communication, and the role of institutions in shaping economic opportunity and social outcomes. She is especially interested in ethical approaches to development, the capabilities approach, and how public messaging influences policy implementation.

Claire's professional experience includes roles with organizations such as FIUTS and Ripple, where she conducted cost-benefit analyses of marketing and outreach initiatives. These experiences strengthened her interest in how intentional resource allocation and institutional decision-making can improve policy outcomes and expand opportunities for communities. Following graduation, she hopes to spend time in Germany studying postwar reconstruction and development policy.
