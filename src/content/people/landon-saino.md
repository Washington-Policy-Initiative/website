Landon is pursuing a degree in political science with specific academic interests in social issues revolving around understanding poverty and its causes. His strongest policy interest is in looking at the intersectionality between economics and policy and the effects that those intersections have on different populations. He has worked on five different research projects at the University of Washington, ranging from topics such as homelessness to artificial intelligence and bot detection.

Landon loves research and learning—whether it be Victorian literature or SQL database creation. He is very excited to share his passion for research with the Initiative.
