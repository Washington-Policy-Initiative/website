Scott W. Allard is the Daniel J. Evans Endowed Professor of Social Policy at the University of Washington's Evans School of Public Policy and Governance, where he served as Associate Dean for Research and Engagement from 2021 to 2025. His research focuses on poverty and inequality, social welfare policy, and urban policy. He is the author of *Places in Need: The Changing Geography of Poverty* and *Out of Reach: Place, Poverty, and the New American Welfare State*. Before joining the Evans School, he held faculty positions at Syracuse University, Brown University, and the University of Chicago. He holds a B.A. from the University of Minnesota and a Ph.D. in political science from the University of Michigan.
