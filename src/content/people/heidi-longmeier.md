Heidi Longmeier (she/her) studies Political Science and Philosophy with a focus in Ethics and a minor in Data Science at the University of Washington, planning to graduate in Spring 2028. Growing up in West Seattle, she saw how the West Seattle Bridge closure isolated her neighborhood and became interested in how the light rail and other forms of public transport could better connect it to the rest of the city.

Heidi canvassed for universal healthcare as a Washington Community Action Network intern and will intern in the marketing department at the Seattle Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce in 2027. At the Washington Policy Initiative, she hopes to build on her outreach experience through local policy research. Outside of academics, Heidi is president of Hounds of Music A Cappella and sings with UW Vocal Jazz.
