Brenna is a Political Science major at the University of Washington, graduating in 2027. She currently works full-time as a Legal Case Manager in personal injury pre-litigation at Brumley Law Firm and serves as the Director of Public Relations for the University of Washington Journal of Political Science. Her interests center on the intersection of public health, law, and public policy, and she plans to attend law school to advocate for individuals and communities facing barriers to justice.

Brenna is most excited to research the intersection of public health and legal rights, particularly how policy can better educate individuals about their rights and access to resources. Through her work in personal injury pre-litigation, she has seen how health outcomes and legal advocacy are deeply connected, and she is interested in exploring policies that improve public awareness, health equity, and access to justice.
