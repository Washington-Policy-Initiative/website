Jessica is studying Finance and Philosophy with a concentration in Ethics at the University of Washington, with plans to graduate in Spring 2029. She is most interested in civic and financial policy, both of which she has developed personal connections to through her community and school board involvement in her hometown of Cle Elum.

In addition to her role with the Washington Policy Initiative, Jessica is an executive member of Venture Capital Club and an initiated member of Chi Omega sorority. She is most excited to research issues that directly affect the citizens of Seattle and Washington State, with the goal of helping create awareness and inspire actionable change across campus, city, and state communities.
