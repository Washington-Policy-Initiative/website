Ally (she/her) is a first year student at the University of Washington planning to pursue Economics and Informatics. She is fascinated by intersectionality between different types of policy and is most interested in civic policy. Through previous experience in community organizing and student government, she became increasingly drawn to the ways policy impacts community relations. A Minnesota native, her interest in civic issues has grown in response to events such as Operation Metro Surge. She hopes to further explore how federal and state policy decisions impact communities throughout Washington State.

Outside of the Washington Policy Initiative, Ally is part of the Outreach Committee for the student magazine HOMEROOM, which focuses on uplifting Asian American visibility through creative expression. She is also a member of the Husky Swim Club.
