Grace is studying Public Policy and International Studies at the University of Washington, with an anticipated graduation in 2029. Prior to beginning her undergraduate studies, she graduated early from high school and spent a gap year in Taiwan studying Mandarin through the U.S. State Department's National Security Language Initiative for Youth. She also served as a Team Leader for the Youth2Youth Investing Program, leading a cross-cultural team of students from Syria, Iraq, and the United States in developing social impact initiatives.

Grace's research interests include education policy, economic policy, and technology policy, with a particular focus on the intersection of innovation, access, and long-term economic growth. Through her academic and leadership experiences, she is interested in exploring how public policy can expand opportunity, improve access, and support stronger long-term outcomes for communities.
