Alazar (he/him) is a student at the University of Washington, double majoring in Public Service and Policy and Political Science with a minor in Data Science, and graduating in the spring of 2029. Growing up in Tukwila and around South Seattle's Ethiopian community, Alazar has developed an interest in education and civic policy. Having experienced Washington public and charter schools alongside public education in Florida, he is motivated to find resourceful, long-term solutions to improve the educational outcomes of marginalized students from Washington and the Pacific Northwest. Outside of the WPI, Alazar volunteers at the Ethiopian Community in Seattle and to support voter registration efforts, spending his free time learning about history and discovering new music.
