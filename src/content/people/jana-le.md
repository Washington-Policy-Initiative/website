Jana (she/her) is a double major in law, societies, and justice and political science, with a concentration in international security. Jana is graduating in Spring of 2027 from the University of Washington. She is interested in the intersections between civic, tech, and international policy.

Outside of her interest and work in research, Jana is passionate about advocating for her community. She is currently serving as the Cultural Chair of UW’s Vietnamese Student Association, and explores the connections between art, fashion, and social justice through her work as a journalist in Fitted. As a first-generation and low-income student, Jana hopes to apply her leadership, research, and writing skills to support marginalized communities.
