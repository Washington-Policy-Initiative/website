Berkley (he/him) is a sophomore studying Construction Management with a minor in Public Policy at UW. He is most interested in civic policy relating to transportation, housing, and urban design and plans to work in a related field.

As well as being in the research cohort of WPI, Berkley is on the executive board of Phi Delta Theta. He is a scholarship recipient of the Association of General Contractors Education Foundation, Building Industry Association of Washington, Master Builders Association of King and Snohomish Counties, and the National Housing Endowment. He is most interested in researching policy that is related to the built environment and is hoping to improve civic health in urban areas.
