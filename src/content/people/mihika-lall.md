Mihika (she/her) is a sophomore studying Law, Societies, and Justice and Environmental Studies at the University of Washington, with plans to graduate in Spring 2028. She is most interested in environmental and housing policy, two issues she connected with growing up in the DC area through her work with Rise n Shine Composting and her outreach to Maryland residents on climate and environmental justice.

In addition to her role with the Washington Policy Initiative, Mihika is a member of Kinspire UW, Kappa Alpha Pi, and UW CLBA. She plans to go to law school and hopes to build a career in environmental justice and public interest law. She is most excited to research how environmental policy shapes daily life for people across Seattle and Washington State, especially the communities most affected by pollution and climate change, and to turn that research into practical solutions local leaders can act on. In her free time, Mihika loves singing, playing guitar, traveling, and hiking.
