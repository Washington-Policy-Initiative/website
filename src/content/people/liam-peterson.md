Liam is an incoming Junior studying Political Science and Informatics with a concentration in Data Science. He founded the Washington Policy Initiative out of passion for exploring how policy impacts people and identifying solutions to policy gaps that affect Washington residents. Some of his prior experience includes data analysis for a rural healthcare organization, interning at the U.S. House of Representatives, and a UW Sociology Department research project focused on unsheltered homelessness in King County.

In summer 2026, Liam was selected as a research scholar for the University of Washington's Summer Institute for Arts and Humanities. He has also worked as a Research Assistant for the Bureau of Governmental Research, a Research Assistant for iSchool PhD student Emily Porter, and an Operations and Compliance Intern for Pico Logic. His research interests include education policy, particularly as it relates to rural communities in Washington State.
