abstract: "This study examines how online kratom retailers verify the age of customers purchasing products for delivery to Washington State. Using a structured website-content coding method, it reviews 30 online retailers and records each retailer's stated minimum purchasing age, use of website age gates, checkout age checks, identification requests, and third-party age or identity verification. Although Washington does not currently have a statewide minimum purchasing age for kratom, 29 of the 30 retailers (96.7%) state that customers must be at least 21, and 90.0% use a website age gate. Stronger verification methods are much less common: only 40.0% use an age-related check at checkout, 13.3% use third-party verification, and 10.0% actively request identification. These findings reveal a clear gap between stating an age restriction and verifying a customer's age. As Washington considers future kratom regulation, the results suggest that setting a minimum purchasing age and defining how online retailers must verify it are separate policy questions that should be addressed together."
