abstract: "An estimated one in four women in the United States will have an abortion by age 45. Although Washington State has strong legal protections for reproductive rights, continuity of care after an abortion remains an overlooked gap in women's healthcare: there is no consistent statewide standard ensuring that patients can access follow-up medical and mental health support. This paper examines the gap between abortion access and ongoing care, with particular attention to patients facing financial, geographic, and healthcare-access barriers. It proposes a voluntary, accessible continuum of post-abortion care that includes recovery education, optional follow-up appointments, coordinated referrals, and appropriate insurance coverage. The paper argues that continuity of care should be recognized as an essential component of comprehensive reproductive healthcare."
