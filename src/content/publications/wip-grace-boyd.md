abstract: "After errors were identified in the Washington Department of Ecology's FY2025 report on Climate Commitment Act (CCA) investments, this study examines whether independent researchers can reproduce the revised report's estimates of lifetime greenhouse gas emissions reductions. From each of the revised report's eight project categories, the study selects the project with the largest reported emissions reduction. It evaluates the transparency of each project using an original rubric that considers published calculation methods, project-specific inputs, state-specific adjustments, and secondary review and verification."
