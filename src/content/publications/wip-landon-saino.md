abstract: "Over the past five years, Washington State has enacted three tax reforms intended to make its tax system more progressive. While these reforms represent progress toward that goal, this paper contends that the state's tax system still rests on a regressive foundation that places a disproportionate burden on low-income families. Rather than continuing to add new structures to the existing system, the paper argues that policymakers should address the underlying features that make Washington's tax system regressive."
