## Who we are

The Washington Policy Initiative is an undergraduate, student-led policy think tank at the University of Washington. We were founded on the belief that students can make meaningful contributions to public policy, and we give the next generation of principled policy leaders a platform to engage with the issues shaping our communities.

Our members come from a wide range of academic disciplines and backgrounds, united by a shared commitment to public service, rigorous research, and constructive dialogue. Through collaborative projects, policy analysis, and engagement with policymakers, community leaders, and subject-matter experts, students gain hands-on experience while bringing fresh perspectives to important policy conversations.

## Our mission

We provide objective, data-driven analysis that informs civic leadership and public policy. We aim to produce high-quality, evidence-based research that identifies critical policy gaps and advances equitable, practical solutions for communities across Washington State.

By bridging academic inquiry and real-world policy challenges, we work to make Washington State policy research accessible and actionable, and to empower residents to advocate for the communities they call home.

## Our nonpartisan commitment

The Washington Policy Initiative is nonpartisan. We don't endorse candidates, parties, or campaigns. Our work follows the evidence: we aim to present data and analysis objectively so readers across the political spectrum can use it to reach their own conclusions.

We believe effective policymaking requires both careful analysis and diverse viewpoints. We strive to build a community where students learn from one another, challenge assumptions, and develop new approaches to complex problems.

## How we're organized

Our work is led by a student Executive Board and carried out by a Research Cohort organized into four policy area teams: [Economic](/areas/economic/), [Education](/areas/education/), [Environmental](/areas/environmental/), and [Civic](/areas/civic/) Policy. Each team is led by a project lead.
