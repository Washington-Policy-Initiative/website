## What members do

Research Cohort members join one of our four policy area teams (Economic, Education, Environmental, or Civic Policy), each led by a project lead, to research policy gaps facing Washington State and turn their findings into research papers and policy memos.

## Who should apply

We encourage students from all academic disciplines and experience levels to apply. One of our core values is fostering learning and growth through mentorship and guidance. Previous experience in research or policy is helpful, but applicants who show exceptional promise, drive, or passion for our goals are encouraged to apply regardless of prior experience.

## How to apply

1. When applications are open, complete the application form linked on this page.
2. Selected applicants are invited to interview with our leadership team.
3. Final membership decisions are made after interviews.
